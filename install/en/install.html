Install the add-on

About 10–15 minutes, on a computer, once. You paste one command, sign in to Google, and then make two clicks that Google requires you to make yourself.

Every step below, shown on screen, in under three minutes, narrated and captioned. The account, code and names are examples.

Before you start

  • A Windows or Mac computer with Chrome or Edge. Installing and using the add-on both need a computer: phone browsers do not show add-ons, and the Gmail app on a phone shows at most a small card beside an open email.
  • The Gmail account you write to the IMA from. If you have several accounts, use that one in every step.
  • The install command the group shared with you (the same command for everyone; there is no personal code).
  • Please read Your data first: it explains what the add-on sends and what it does not.

Step 1: run the installer

  1. Open a command window. Windows: press Start, type PowerShell and open it. Mac: press ⌘+Space, type Terminal and open it.
  2. Paste the install command into that window and press Enter. Copy the whole command, exactly as it is.
  3. Sign in to Google. A browser window opens. Choose the Gmail account you write to the IMA from. Google shows a screen for a tool called clasp with nine permissions: leave them all ticked and press Allow.
    clasp is Google's own tool for creating Apps Script projects. The installer uses it only to create your own copy of the add-on, and removes the sign-in at the end. It does not read your email. What the installer does
  4. Only on a new account: a page opens asking you to turn on Google Apps Script API. Turn it on and go back to the command window. It can take a few minutes for Google to notice; the installer keeps trying by itself.
  5. When the installer says Done, it opens your new add-on project in the browser. You can close the command window.

Google will email you "You allowed clasp access…" and later a similar notice for your own copy. Both are normal.

Step 2: the two clicks Google requires

  1. Install. In the project page that opened: click the blue Deploy button, then Test deployments, then Install. If a small window appears, wait a few seconds for it to load.
  2. Allow. Open Gmail and press F5 to reload. Open any email and click the new add-on icon in the panel on the right. Press Authorize access and choose your account.
    • Google shows "Google hasn't verified this app". That is expected: it is your own personal copy, not an app from a store. Press Advanced, then Go to … (unsafe).
    • Press Allow. What each permission is for

If the panel stays empty after you allow: open the menu (⋮) at the top of the panel and choose Refresh, or reload Gmail (F5).

Step 3: the welcome screens

The first time you open the add-on, it asks four short things. The optional ones have a Skip button.

  1. Language and your name. Your name is used only to sign emails you write with the add-on.
  2. About you (optional): career stage, field, workplace. Every question has "prefer not to say". Why we ask
  3. Gemini suggestions (optional). During the pilot we suggest you skip this. More
  4. Your history. If this is your first install, press This is my first time. If you used the add-on before, press Restore my history.

That's it. The add-on now checks your mailbox once a day by itself. Next: Using it.

More

Adding a Gemini key (optional)

The add-on works fully without Gemini. With your own key, Gemini suggests a rating for each reply; you always make the final choice. Read what Gemini receives first.

  1. Open aistudio.google.com/apikey, sign in, press Create API key and copy the key.
  2. In the add-on open the menu (⋮), choose Settings, and next to Gemini press Key.
  3. Paste the key into Gemini API key and press Save.
If the installer does not work: installing by hand

You do not need to sign up first: your account joins the group automatically the first time the add-on contacts the group's server. Then:

  1. Open a new Apps Script project, signed in with the account you write to the IMA from.
  2. Press the gear (Project Settings) on the left and tick Show "appsscript.json" manifest file in editor. Go back to the editor (the <> icon).
  3. On the latest release page, open Code.gs and appsscript.json. Copy each file's whole text over the file of the same name in the editor, then press Save (the disk icon).
  4. Continue with Step 2 and Step 3 above.
Something went wrong
  • The installer says the server did not accept your account. Usually another Google account was chosen: run the installer again and pick the account you write to the IMA from. If it happens again, write to us.
  • The installer keeps waiting at "Google Apps Script API". Make sure the switch on that page is on, for the same account you signed in with. Google can take several minutes.
  • I don't see the add-on icon in Gmail. Open an email (not just the inbox) and make sure the right-hand panel is open (the small arrow at the screen's edge). Check that Step 2's Install showed "Installed", then reload Gmail.
  • An error says a library is missing, or that I don't have permission. Your account was not given access to the group's files. Write to us with the Gmail address you use.
  • Settings shows an upload problem with "not authorized". Your sign-in expired, or your account was removed from the group. Your records are kept on your side and are sent automatically once this is fixed. Write to us.
  • Settings says your membership is paused for a review. This happens automatically after unusual activity: very many records in one day, or the add-on installed on more than 5 computers within 90 days. The group's admins look at it; your records are kept on your side and are sent once the pause is lifted. You can write to us to explain.
  • The add-on asks me to update. See Updates. Nothing is lost while you wait.

A new computer, or installing again

Run the installer command again (the same command as the first time). Each install on another computer counts as a new copy of the add-on; more than 5 within 90 days pauses your membership for a review. On the last welcome screen press Restore my history, or later: Settings → Advanced → Restore history from the log. The conversations you already tracked come back, so you are not asked about them twice. Your Gemini key, your name and your style choices are not restored: enter them again.

Updates

Almost every update reaches you by itself: you don't need to do anything. If the panel looks old after an update, use Refresh in the panel's menu (⋮).

Rarely, the add-on's own file changes too. Then the add-on shows a notice with a download link. The simplest way to update is to run your installer command again; it updates your existing copy.

Removing the add-on

Open your add-on project (script.google.com, "IMA Reply Tracking"), click Deploy → Test deployments → Uninstall. You can also delete the project. From then on nothing more is sent. Records already sent stay in the sealed log; see Stopping, and removing your records.

What the installer does, and how to check it

The installer is three plain-text files that you or anyone can read. It writes only into one folder in your home directory (.ima-reply-tracking), installs nothing system-wide, never reads your email, and removes the Google sign-in at the end. Each file is checked against a published fingerprint before it runs.
