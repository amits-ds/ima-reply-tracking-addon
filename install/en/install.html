Install the add-on, step by step

About 15–20 minutes, on a computer, once. Follow the steps in order; each one says what you will see.

Watch the installation

The whole process on screen, in under three minutes. Recorded before the Gemini key step (Step 3) was added; the account and names are examples. The written steps below are the current ones.

Before you start

  • A Windows or Mac computer with Chrome or Edge. The add-on works only on a computer, not on a phone.
  • The Gmail account you write to the IMA from. If you have several Google accounts, choose that one every time Google asks.
  • Please read Your data first: what the add-on sends, and what it never sends.

Step 1: run the installer

  1. Open a command window.
    • Windows: click Start (or press the Windows key), type PowerShell, and click Windows PowerShell. A blue or black window opens with a blinking cursor.
    • Mac: press ⌘+Space, type Terminal, and press Enter. A white or black window opens with a blinking cursor.
  2. Copy the command for your computer (press the Copy button under it):

    Windows

    $r=Invoke-WebRequest -UseBasicParsing 'https://raw.githubusercontent.com/amits-ds/ima-reply-tracking-addon/main/install/install.ps1'; & ([scriptblock]::Create([Text.Encoding]::UTF8.GetString($r.RawContentStream.ToArray())))

    Mac

    curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/amits-ds/ima-reply-tracking-addon/main/install/install.sh -o "$HOME/reply-tracking-install.sh" && bash "$HOME/reply-tracking-install.sh"
  3. Paste it into the command window and press Enter. Windows: right-click inside the window (or press Ctrl+V). Mac: press ⌘+V. The installer now works through six numbered steps, [1/6] to [6/6]. The first time, it downloads a private copy of a tool it needs (Node.js, 30–40 MB, into one folder; nothing is installed system-wide), so wait a minute.
  4. Sign in to Google ([2/6]). A browser tab opens.
    1. Choose the Gmail account you write to the IMA from.
    2. Google shows a page for a tool called clasp that asks for several permissions. Leave everything ticked and press Allow (scroll down if you don't see it).
    3. The tab says you can close it. Go back to the command window.
    clasp is Google's own tool for creating Apps Script projects. The installer uses it only to create your own copy of the add-on, and removes the sign-in at the end ([5/6]). It does not read your email. What the installer does
  5. Only if a page about "Google Apps Script API" opens (first time on an account): click the switch so it turns On, then go back to the command window and wait. Google can take a few minutes to notice; the installer keeps trying by itself.
  6. The installer finishes ([6/6]): it opens your new add-on project in the browser and prints the two last clicks (Step 2 below). Then it asks Open the Gemini key page in your browser now? (y/N): type y and press Enter (you will need it in Step 3). When it says Done, you can close the command window.

Google emails you "You allowed clasp access…", and later a similar notice for your own copy. Both are normal.

Step 2: install it in Gmail (two clicks Google requires you to make yourself)

  1. Install. In the add-on project page that opened (Apps Script, titled "IMA Reply Tracking"):
    1. Click the blue Deploy button at the top right, then Test deployments.
    2. A window opens. Wait a few seconds for it to load, then click Install and then Done.
  2. Allow.
    1. Open Gmail (the same account) and press F5 to reload it.
    2. Open any email. On the right edge of Gmail there is a narrow column of icons; click the new add-on icon. (If you don't see the column, click the small arrow at the bottom right corner.)
    3. The panel shows Authorize access: click it and choose your account.
    4. Google shows "Google hasn't verified this app". That is expected: it is your own personal copy, not an app from a store. Click Advanced, then Go to IMA Reply Tracking (unsafe).
    5. Google lists what the add-on may do. If there are checkboxes, tick Select all. Press Continue or Allow. What each permission is for

If the panel stays empty after you allow: open the menu (⋮) at the top of the panel and choose Refresh, or reload Gmail (F5).

Step 3: get your Gemini key (recommended)

With a Gemini key, the add-on suggests a rating for each IMA reply, and can rewrite your emails in your own style. You always make the final choice. The key is free and personal; it stays in your own copy of the add-on and is never sent to the group.

Privacy, read before you continue. With a key, the text of the IMA's replies (and your own messages in those conversations) is sent to Google's Gemini. On Google's free tier, Google may use what is sent to improve its products. Replies can contain personal or medical details. If that is a problem for you, skip this step: everything else works, and you rate replies yourself. What Gemini receives
  1. Open aistudio.google.com/apikey (or use the tab the installer opened). Sign in with any Google account; the same one is simplest.
  2. Only the first time: a "Welcome to Google AI Studio" page appears. Tick the box confirming you are over 18 (the second box, for emails, is up to you) and press Continue.
  3. On the API Keys page, click Create API key at the top right.
  4. A window "Create a new key" opens. Leave the name as it is. Under the project, keep what is selected (if the list is empty, choose to create a new project; any name is fine). Click Create key.
  5. The new key appears in the list (only its last few characters are shown). Click the copy icon in its row; the whole key, a long code starting with AIza, is copied. Keep this tab open until you have pasted it (Step 4).

Do not share the key with anyone. If it leaks, delete it on the same page and create a new one.

Step 4: the welcome screens

Go back to Gmail and the add-on panel. The first time, it shows four short screens.

  1. Welcome: choose the Language and type Your name, for the sign-off of emails. Press Continue.
  2. About you (optional): career stage, field, workplace. Every question has "prefer not to say". Press Save and continue, or Skip. Why we ask
  3. Gemini suggestions (optional): click in the field Gemini API key, paste the key from Step 3 (Ctrl+V, or ⌘+V on a Mac), and press Continue. If you skipped Step 3, press Skip.
  4. Your history: first install? Press This is my first time. Used the add-on before? Press Restore my history.

Done. The add-on now checks your mailbox by itself (every two hours, and a full check once a day). Next: Using it.

Adding or changing the Gemini key later: menu (⋮) → Settings → Key next to Gemini → paste into Gemini API key → Save.

More

If the installer does not work: installing by hand

You do not need to sign up first: your account joins the group automatically the first time the add-on contacts the group's server. Then:

  1. Open a new Apps Script project, signed in with the account you write to the IMA from.
  2. Press the gear (Project Settings) on the left and tick Show "appsscript.json" manifest file in editor. Go back to the editor (the <> icon).
  3. On the latest release page, open Code.gs and appsscript.json. Copy each file's whole text over the file of the same name in the editor, then press Save (the disk icon).
  4. Continue with Step 2.
Something went wrong
  • The command window shows red text, or "ERROR". Take a screenshot of the window and send it to us. Running the same command again is safe.
  • The installer says the server did not accept your account. Usually another Google account was chosen: run the installer again and pick the account you write to the IMA from. If it happens again, write to us.
  • The installer keeps waiting at "Google Apps Script API". Make sure the switch on that page is on, for the same account you signed in with. Google can take several minutes.
  • I don't see the add-on icon in Gmail. Open an email (not just the inbox) and make sure the right-hand column is open (the small arrow at the screen's edge). Check that Step 2's Install was done, then reload Gmail.
  • An error says a library is missing, or that I don't have permission. Write to us with the Gmail address you use.
  • Gemini suggestions don't appear. Check the key in Settings (it should say Rating suggestions are on). The free tier has daily limits; the add-on keeps working without suggestions until the next day.
  • Settings shows an upload problem with "not authorized". Your sign-in expired, or your account was removed from the group. Your records are kept on your side and are sent automatically once this is fixed. Write to us.
  • Settings says your membership is paused for a review. This happens automatically after unusual activity: very many records in one day, or the add-on installed on more than 5 computers within 90 days. The group's admins look at it; your records are kept on your side and are sent once the pause is lifted. You can write to us to explain.
  • The add-on asks me to update. See Updates. Nothing is lost while you wait.

A new computer, or installing again

Run the installer command again (the same command as the first time), with the same Google account. It finds the add-on copy you already have in your Google account and updates it, on any computer, so nothing else is needed. Only if that copy was deleted does it create a new one; a new copy counts toward the limit (more than 5 within 90 days pauses your membership for a review), and then on the last welcome screen press Restore my history, or later: Settings → Advanced → Restore history from the log. The conversations you already tracked come back, so you are not asked about them twice. Your Gemini key, your name and your style choices are not restored: enter them again.

Updates

Almost every update reaches you by itself: you don't need to do anything. If the panel looks old after an update, use Refresh in the panel's menu (⋮).

Rarely, the add-on's own file changes too. Then the add-on shows a notice that links here. To update, run the installer command again, on any computer, signing in with the same Google account:

$r=Invoke-WebRequest -UseBasicParsing 'https://raw.githubusercontent.com/amits-ds/ima-reply-tracking-addon/main/install/install.ps1'; & ([scriptblock]::Create([Text.Encoding]::UTF8.GetString($r.RawContentStream.ToArray())))
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/amits-ds/ima-reply-tracking-addon/main/install/install.sh -o "$HOME/reply-tracking-install.sh" && bash "$HOME/reply-tracking-install.sh"

It finds your copy of the add-on and updates it. You don't repeat the clicks in Gmail or the welcome screens, and nothing you saved is lost. When it says Done, reload Gmail (F5).

Removing the add-on

Open your add-on project (script.google.com, "IMA Reply Tracking"), click Deploy → Test deployments → Uninstall. You can also delete the project. From then on nothing more is sent. Records already sent stay in the sealed log; see Stopping, and removing your records.

What the installer does, and how to check it

The installer is three plain-text files that you or anyone can read. It writes only into one folder in your home directory (.ima-reply-tracking), installs nothing system-wide, never reads your email, and removes the Google sign-in at the end. Each file is checked against a published fingerprint before it runs.
