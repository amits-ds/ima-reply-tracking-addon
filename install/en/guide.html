Using the add-on

Most weeks it takes a few minutes. The add-on checks your mailbox by itself every two hours for new replies, and runs a full check once a day. You only act when something waits for you.

The whole tour in under two minutes, narrated and captioned. The names and emails are examples.

The home screen

Open Gmail and click the add-on icon in the right-hand panel. The home screen has two parts:

  • Needs you: replies from the IMA to rate, and new conversations with the IMA to sort. When there are any, the button at the bottom reads Review 3 items.
  • Your inquiries: how many are waiting for the IMA, how many wait for you (The IMA replied last), and Tracking 9 of 11 conversations with the IMA in your mailbox.

Check now checks your mailbox immediately instead of waiting for the next automatic check.

A review, step by step

Press the review button. The add-on goes through the waiting items one by one:

  1. New conversations (New conversations with the IMA): for each, pick a topic if it is your inquiry, or choose Not an inquiry. Not sure? Leave it on Decide later. The last topic in the list, "Not sure / other", is fine when nothing fits.
  2. Replies (Rate the reply): read the IMA's reply and tap the rating that fits. The tap saves it and moves to the next item.

You can leave an item with Skip for now or stop with Stop review; what you skipped comes back next time, and what you did is saved.

The four ratings

Rate what the reply says, not how polite it is. The full definitions are one tap away on every reply (Full definitions (the group's rubric)).

RatingWhen
HelpfulA relevant, detailed answer that resolves your inquiry.
UnhelpfulA partial or general answer: it touches your question but does not resolve it.
Non-answerOnly confirms receipt, or refers you on without substance: "received", "forwarded for handling", an auto-reply.
RefusalAn explicit refusal to help (for example: it is not the union's role).

When the IMA does not answer

If 14 days pass after your last message with no reply, the add-on records "no reply" by itself and tells you after your next review, under Recorded automatically. The conversation stays open: if the IMA answers later, you rate the reply as usual.

If you got your answer another way (by phone, in a meeting), press Resolved, so it is not counted as unanswered.

Writing a new inquiry

  1. On the home screen press New inquiry.
  2. Choose the recipients, a topic and a template (or No template (I will write it)).
  3. Press Open in Gmail. The text opens as a Gmail draft: edit it there, add attachments if needed, and send it as usual. Once it is sent, the add-on tracks it by itself.

Drafts you have not sent yet are listed on the home screen. You can also send without Gmail's editor with Send from here; the add-on asks you to confirm before anything is sent.

Inquiries you write in Gmail without the add-on are found by the daily check and offered to you as new conversations.

Following up

Open the inquiry (All inquiries, then the conversation) and press Send a follow-up. The add-on prepares a reminder from the group's template in the same conversation, and sends only after you confirm with Yes, send now.

Changing a rating

Open the conversation, find the reply in its timeline and press Change. The new rating is recorded with today's date; the earlier one stays in the log, and the latest one counts.

From an open email

When you open an email from the IMA, the add-on panel shows that conversation. If it is not tracked yet, it asks Is this one of your inquiries? and you can press Track this conversation.

Settings

Open the menu (⋮) and choose Settings. There you can change the language, your name and your About you answers, turn the Daily check and the Weekly reminder email on or off, and add a Gemini key (how).

At the bottom of every screen, All saved to the group log means everything has reached the log. If records are waiting, they are kept on your computer and sent automatically.

Questions

Does the add-on read all my mail?

It searches your mailbox for conversations with IMA addresses (ima.org.il and the few personal addresses on the group's list) and reads only those. Nothing about other mail is recorded. What is sent

Can the group read my emails?

No. The log receives dates, addresses, topics, your ratings and the IMA's signed header lines, not the text of your emails. If copies are ever needed, we will ask you first (how). What exactly is in a record

Does the IMA know I use it?

The add-on never contacts the IMA. Emails you send with it are ordinary emails from you.

I rated something by mistake, or tracked the wrong conversation.

Change the rating as described above. For a conversation tracked by mistake, write to us: imareplytracker@gmail.com.

I was away for weeks. Did I miss anything?

No. Times are taken from the emails themselves, not from when you open the add-on, so catching up later gives the same data.

How do I stop?

Remove the add-on (how). Nothing more is sent from then on. What happens to records already sent
